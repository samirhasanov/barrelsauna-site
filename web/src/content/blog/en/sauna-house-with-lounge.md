A sauna house with a lounge is a separate building in your garden that holds far more than a hot room: a place to relax, a shower and WC, and — if you want — a steam room, hammam, massage room and a hot tub or pool. At Barrel Sauna we call this format the **cottage sauna**. It is built for 4–20 people and costs roughly 10,000–50,000 AZN. Below we cover which rooms you can combine, how to size it and how the design is prepared.

## How a sauna house differs from a standard sauna

A barrel or cube sauna is a single hot room: you go in, warm up, and step out into the garden to cool down. A sauna house is a complete space. Guests change there, sit in the lounge with tea between rounds, shower, and maybe book a massage. Everything happens under one roof, whatever the weather.

From the outside a cottage sauna looks like a small house — straight walls, a pitched roof, big windows. That makes the interior easy to plan and lets the building sit naturally next to a villa.

## Which rooms can a sauna house include?

The real strength of the cottage format is that you choose the rooms. Options include:

- **Sauna room** — a classic Finnish sauna with dry heat.
- **Steam room** — softer, more humid heat.
- **Salt room** — a calm space lined with Himalayan salt panels.
- **Turkish hammam** — warm marble and a foam-massage ritual.
- **Venik room** — a separate hot room for traditional whisking with birch or oak bundles.
- **Massage room** — for unwinding after a session.
- **Hot or cold tub, or a pool** — for contrast bathing.
- **Bathroom / WC** — shower and toilet.
- **Lounge** — sofa, table, tea corner, TV: the living room of the whole sauna house.

You don't need everything. For most families "sauna + shower/WC + lounge" is plenty; other rooms are added according to budget and how you plan to use the house.

## How many people should it hold?

A cottage sauna can be built for 4 to 20 people. To size it, answer four questions:

1. **How many people will sit in the sauna at the same time?** This sets the size of the hot room and the heater output.
2. **How many people should fit in the lounge?** If you entertain, the lounge should be larger than the sauna.
3. **How many showers and toilets do you need?** For big groups, two showers make a real difference.
4. **What might you add later?** If a tub or pool comes later, reserve the space now.

## What drives the price

A cottage sauna costs roughly **10,000–50,000 AZN**. The range is wide because two sauna houses can be completely different projects. The main factors are:

- total floor area and number of rooms;
- wet zones such as a hammam, steam room or pool, which need extra waterproofing and engineering;
- interior timber: standard linden, or cedar, abachi or teak;
- heater type and output (Harvia, SAWO, Teplodar, TMF, Fanlan);
- roofing, windows, lighting and décor (salt panels, decorative stone, log-cut décor, LED).

## How a sauna house is built

The cottage uses the same build system as Barrel Sauna's other outdoor models:

- **Exterior cladding** — water-resistant, thermo-treated larch (listvenica), known locally as "ship timber".
- **Multilayer walls** — mineral stone wool plus a special membrane that keeps moisture and steam away from the timber frame.
- **Interior** — linden by default; cedar, abachi, African abachi or teak on request.
- **Roof** — ondulin or profiled metal sheet options.

Insulation and ventilation between wet zones (shower, hammam, pool) and hot rooms matter a lot, and we solve them at the design stage. Foundation, water supply, drainage and electrics are planned at the same stage.

## 3D design: see it before it's built

## FAQ

### How much does a sauna house with a lounge cost?

Roughly 10,000–50,000 AZN. The final figure depends on floor area, number of rooms, wet zones, timber and heater.

### Does a sauna house need a pool?

No. Many clients are happy with a cold plunge tub or a shower. A pool is a great addition for contrast bathing and summer, but it's optional.

### How many people can a sauna house hold?

Anywhere from 4 to 20. Sizing is based on how many people will be in the sauna and in the lounge at the same time.

### Why do I need a 3D design?

It lets you see and change the layout, interior and dimensions before construction starts, avoiding costly changes later.
