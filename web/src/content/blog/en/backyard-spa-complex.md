A backyard spa complex brings a sauna, steam room, hammam, pool or plunge tub, showers and a relaxation area together in one open-air wellness zone. Barrel Sauna describes it as a "five-star hotel private spa in your own yard": built for 4–15 people at roughly 15,000–45,000 AZN. The most important work happens before construction starts — zoning. Here's how to plan it.

## Spa complex vs cottage sauna

A [cottage sauna](/en/cottage-sauna) puts every room inside one closed house. A spa complex is more open and spacious: hot rooms, pool, terrace and lounge form a single space across part of your plot. You step out of the sauna straight into the pool, then rest on a covered terrace — just like a hotel spa, only at home.

A complex can include:

- a Finnish sauna room;
- a steam room;
- a salt room;
- a Turkish hammam;
- a venik (whisking) room;
- a massage room;
- a hot or cold tub, or a pool;
- showers and WC;
- a lounge or covered terrace.

## Zoning: the key to a spa that works

In a good spa complex, people move from zone to zone in a logical order and never cross the whole garden with wet feet. We divide the space into four zones.

### 1. Hot zone

Sauna, steam room, hammam, venik room. These sit close together to reduce heat loss and keep heater, ventilation and electrical runs short and simple.

### 2. Cold zone

Cold plunge tub, pool or contrast shower. The rule is simple: only a few steps from the sauna door to cold water, along a non-slip surface.

### 3. Relaxation zone

Covered terrace or lounge with sofa, table and tea corner. It sits slightly apart from the hot rooms but within sight, so steam and noise don't drift in.

### 4. Technical and hygiene zone

Showers, WC, changing area, plus a plant room for pool filters, the water heater and the electrical panel. Hidden from guests, but easy to reach for maintenance.

## Choosing the spot on your plot

When we choose a location we look at:

1. **Terrain.** Flat ground keeps things simple; on a slope we plan terraces and steps.
2. **Privacy.** The pool and terrace should be screened from neighbours and the street.
3. **Sun and view.** A terrace facing the evening sun or the mountains changes the whole experience.
4. **Utilities.** Distance to water, drainage and power has a direct effect on cost.
5. **The path from the house.** A short, lit path matters, especially in winter.

## Home spa vs hotel spa

| Factor | Villa / home | Hotel / guest house |
|---|---|---|
| Capacity | Usually 4–8 people | Up to 8–15 people |
| Core rooms | Sauna, tub or pool, lounge | Several hot rooms, pool, separate showers and WC |
| Priority | Comfort, view, privacy | Guest flow, durability, easy cleaning |
| Use | Weekends, family and friends | Daily, many guests |
| Plant room | Simple | Stronger filtration and water supply |

For hotels we also plan the guest route — entrance, changing, shower, hot zone, cold zone, relaxation — so flows never cross.

## What affects the price

An outdoor spa complex costs roughly **15,000–45,000 AZN**. The main factors are:

- number and size of hot rooms;
- whether you add a pool or tub — [hot tubs and jacuzzis](/en/hot-tubs-jacuzzi) can be chosen separately;
- interior timber (linden, cedar, abachi, teak) and décor (Himalayan salt panels, decorative stone, LED lighting);
- heaters — Harvia, SAWO or Saunabee, wood-fired or electric;
- terrace, canopy and landscaping;
- length of utility runs and the terrain.

## Materials and build

The hot rooms are built like Barrel Sauna's outdoor saunas: water-resistant, thermo-treated larch outside, mineral stone wool and a moisture-blocking membrane in the walls, and linden inside by default. Roofing options include ondulin or profiled metal sheet.

## Planning for contrast bathing

The real pleasure of a spa complex is moving between hot and cold: sauna, then a cold tub or pool, then rest. To make that cycle easy we add towel hooks at the hot-zone exit, a bench by the cold zone and space for water and tea in the lounge. Sharp temperature changes aren't for everyone — if you have a heart condition or other health concerns, check with your doctor first.

## FAQ

### How much does a backyard spa complex cost?

Roughly 15,000–45,000 AZN. The final price depends on the rooms, the pool, materials and the site.

### How much space does a spa complex need?

It depends on the rooms you choose and the pool size. After seeing the site we propose the best layout and dimensions.

### Is it suitable for a hotel or guest house?

Yes. A complex can be built for up to 15 people and makes a strong addition to a guest offer.

### Is a pool essential?

No. A cold or hot tub, or a contrast shower, works too and is simpler in both space and budget.

### Can the spa be used in winter?

Yes. The hot rooms are fully insulated, and the lounge can be covered or enclosed.
