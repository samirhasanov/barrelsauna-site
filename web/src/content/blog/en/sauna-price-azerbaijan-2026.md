Roomier cabin and elliptical saunas run 8,000–15,000 AZN, while multi-room cottage saunas range from 10,000 to 50,000 AZN. ## What affects the price of a sauna

### 1. Model and shape

Shape sets the complexity of the build and how much material it takes. Barrels and cubes are compact, which keeps them in the most affordable bracket. A cabin sauna adds a panoramic glass front; a cottage sauna adds several separate rooms — both push the price up.

### 2. Size and capacity

Within a single model, price still scales with size: a two-person barrel and a long six-person barrel differ in materials and labour. Sizing to your real needs is one of the easiest ways to control cost.

### 3. Interior wood

Linden is the standard interior. Choosing thermo linden, cedar, abachi, African abachi or teak changes the price. Each has its strengths — abachi makes the most comfortable benches, cedar is prized for its aroma.

### 4. Stove

Electric or wood-burning, the heater's output and the brand all affect the total. We supply and install Harvia, SAWO and Saunabee stoves and size them to the sauna's volume. See the options on our [sauna stoves](/en/sauna-heaters) page.

### 5. Extra rooms and features

For cottage saunas and SPA complexes, the room list is the biggest price driver. Options include:

- sauna room and steam room;
- salt room;
- Turkish hamam;
- broom (venik) room;
- massage room;
- hot or cold tub, or a pool;
- bathroom / WC;
- rest room or lounge.

That's why the cottage sauna range is so wide — from 10,000 to 50,000 AZN.

### 6. Roofing

Tegola (bitumen shingles) over a roofing-felt layer gives double protection. Some models can be finished in ondulin or profiled metal sheet instead, which affects both the look and the price.

### 7. Decor and lighting

LED lighting, Himalayan salt panels, decorative stone and log-cut decor make a sauna special, but each adds to the budget. They can also be added in stages.

### 8. Site preparation

On top of the sauna itself, budget for the base (concrete slab, paving or screw piles), a power line and, if needed, a water supply. These costs depend on the condition of your site.

## Example scenarios: where your budget might land

To make the ranges more concrete, here are a few typical scenarios:

- **A compact sauna for two.** A two-person barrel or cube, standard linden, electric heater, minimal decor — usually toward the lower end of 6,000–12,000 AZN.
- **A family sauna.** A six-person barrel or cube with abachi benches, LED lighting and a wood-burning stove — these choices move the price toward the upper end of the range.
- **A villa with a view.** A 4–8 person cabin sauna with a panoramic glass front, within 8,000–15,000 AZN depending on size and interior wood.
- **A guesthouse or hotel.** A cottage sauna or outdoor SPA complex with a sauna, hamam, salt room and lounge — 10,000–50,000 AZN for a cottage or 15,000–45,000 AZN for a SPA complex, depending on the rooms.

These are only a guide; your own quote is calculated from your specific choices.

## How to save without cutting corners

- **Choose the right size.** An oversized sauna costs more and takes longer to heat.
- **Start with standard linden.** It's resin-free, comfortable and well balanced on price.
- **Use abachi only for benches.** Linden walls with abachi benches give top comfort at a sensible cost.
- **Add extras in stages.** Decor and some accessories can come later.
- **Prepare the site early.** Planning the base and wiring in advance avoids rework.

## Cheap sauna or the right sauna?

The most expensive thing in a sauna is a mistake you have to fix later. When comparing quotes, look beyond the headline number:

- What is the exterior made of, and does it handle moisture?
- Are there insulation and a vapour membrane in the walls?
- How many layers does the roof have?
- Is the heater sized to the room's volume?
