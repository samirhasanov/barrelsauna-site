The best sauna for a country house is one sized for your family, placed in the right spot in the garden and fitted with a stove that suits how you live. A family of 2–4 is usually well served by a barrel or cube sauna (6,000–12,000 AZN); 4–8 people fit an oval or cabin sauna (8,000–15,000 AZN); and if you want a lounge and shower, a cottage sauna (10,000–50,000 AZN) is the answer. Here's how to choose, step by step.

## Step 1. Match the model to your family

The most common mistake is buying for the day "the whole extended family visits". In reality, it's mostly your own household using the sauna at weekends — and every extra cubic metre costs money and takes longer to heat.

| Model | Capacity | Price guide | When it suits a country house |
|---|---|---|---|
| Barrel sauna | 2–6 people | 6,000–12,000 AZN | Small to mid-size family, compact garden, fast heat-up |
| Cube sauna | 2–6 people | 6,000–12,000 AZN | Modern house, large glass front for the view |
| Oval (semi-circular / elliptical) sauna | 4–8 people | 8,000–15,000 AZN | Larger family, front terrace, roomier interior |
| Cabin sauna | 4–8 people | 8,000–15,000 AZN | Panoramic glass facade, hosting guests |
| Cottage sauna | 4–20 people | 10,000–50,000 AZN | Sauna + lounge + shower/WC — a full bathhouse |
| Outdoor SPA complex | 4–15 people | 15,000–45,000 AZN | Several rooms: hamam, salt room, pool and more |

All shapes are in our [outdoor saunas](/en/outdoor-saunas) section.

**Rule of thumb:**

- 2–4 people, frequent use → barrel or cube.
- 5–8 people, regular guests → oval or cabin.
- You want a separate space to sit, have tea and shower after the sauna → cottage sauna.

## Step 2. Pick the right spot

Placement affects comfort as much as price does. Think about:

- **Distance to the house.** The path from sauna to house should be short and easy — especially in winter. With a wood stove, make sure smoke won't drift towards the house windows.
- **The view.** Point the glass door or panoramic wall at the garden, mountains or forest, not the neighbour's fence.
- **Privacy.** A corner that can't be seen from the street or next door is far more relaxing.
- **Drainage.** Never place the sauna in a dip where rainwater collects.
- **Cool-down zone.** Leave room nearby for a shower, plunge tub or hot tub.
- **Utilities.** For an electric stove, plan how far the power line has to run.

## Step 3. The base

An outdoor sauna doesn't need heavy foundations, but a level, solid base is essential:

- **Concrete slab** — the most reliable, long-lasting option.
- **Paving stones** — economical if you already have a paved area.
- **Screw piles** — handy on slopes, soft ground, or when you'd rather not pour concrete.

What matters is that the surface is level, doesn't flood and can carry the sauna's weight. Send us photos and we'll tell you which option fits your site.

## Step 4. Wood or electric?

| Criterion | Wood-burning stove | Electric heater |
|---|---|---|
| Atmosphere | Classic bathhouse, live fire, wood scent | Clean and quiet |
| Power line | Not needed | Needs an adequate supply |
| Everyday use | You prepare firewood and tend the fire | Push-button, easy temperature control |
| For a country house | Weak power supply or occasional visits | Frequent use and convenience |

Country houses often have a weak power supply, which makes a wood stove the more practical choice. Barrel Sauna offers Harvia, SAWO and Saunabee stoves — see them on the [sauna heaters](/en/sauna-heaters) page.

## Step 5. Add a hot tub or plunge pool

The biggest perk of a garden sauna is cooling off outdoors. Sauna plus a hot or cold tub is the favourite combination:

- **Wooden hot tub or plunge tub** — right next to the sauna, under the open sky.
- **Jacuzzi** — relaxation with hydromassage.
- **Container sauna + jacuzzi** — both in one ready-made module.

See the options in [hot tubs & jacuzzi](/en/hot-tubs-jacuzzi). In our Qəbələ project, a barrel sauna was installed together with a wooden hot tub, creating a complete retreat even on a snowy winter day.

## Step 6. Budget for the whole picture

Beyond the sauna itself, allow for:

- the base (if there's no ready pad);
- a power line (for an electric heater);
- a hot tub or plunge tub (this can also be added later);
- accessories and lighting.

With a cottage sauna, the price depends on the rooms you choose: sauna, steam room, salt room, Turkish hamam, broom (venik) room, lounge, shower/WC and so on.

## Using a garden sauna through the seasons

- **Summer.** Bathe in the evening once the heat eases. Cooling off in the garden or a cold plunge afterwards is a real treat.
- **Autumn.** Before the rainy season, check the roof and the seal on doors and windows; on a barrel, inspect the steel bands.
- **Winter.** The best season for an outdoor sauna — a hot sauna in a snowy garden is something special. Plan where to clear snow from the path.
- **Spring.** After a long break, air the sauna out, wipe the benches and check the body.

After every session, leave the door open so the sauna dries out — it helps the wood last longer. Anyone with health conditions should check with their doctor before using a sauna.

## Useful extras for a country-house sauna

- **Path and lighting.** A stone or timber path from the house to the sauna, with soft lighting for evenings.
- **Firewood storage.** If you chose a wood stove, a small covered log store.
- **Space for clothes and towels.** A front terrace or porch solves this neatly.
- **Accessories.** Bucket, ladle, thermometer, hats and decor make the sauna complete and comfortable.

Small details, but they make everyday use of a garden sauna much easier.

## FAQ

### What's the most affordable sauna for a country house?

The barrel and cube saunas — 2–6 people, 6,000–12,000 AZN. Both are compact, heat up quickly and fit even small gardens.

### How close to the house can the sauna go?

It's a standalone outdoor building, so it doesn't affect your house walls. Choose the spot based on convenience, view, drainage and where wood-stove smoke will drift.

### Will it survive at a country house we rarely visit in winter?

The body is water-resistant larch, the roof has a double shield (tegola shingles over roofing felt), and on barrel saunas the adjustable steel bands compensate for natural wood shrinkage. After a long break, simply air it out and give it a check.

### Can a sauna go without a foundation?

It needs a level, solid, well-drained base — a concrete slab, paving or screw piles. Heavy foundations aren't required.

### Can I order a sauna together with a hot tub?

Yes. We can plan the sauna together with a hot tub or plunge tub, and the container format combines both in a single module.
