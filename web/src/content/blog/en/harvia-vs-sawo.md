Harvia vs SAWO? The short answer: both are quality Finnish brands, and a correctly sized heater from either will serve you well for years. **If you want a wood burning stove, go with Harvia; if you want a tower heater with a big stone load or one built into the bench, look closely at SAWO.** Otherwise the choice comes down to room volume, design and how you want to control it. Barrel Sauna sells and installs both brands, so this comparison doesn't lean either way.

## The two brands in brief

**Harvia** is one of the best-known Finnish makers of sauna heaters and equipment. Its range is very broad: electric heaters, wood stoves, combi heaters with a steam function, steam generators, infrared panels and control units.

**SAWO** is also Finnish, and its core strength is **electric heaters**. The design is distinctive: cylindrical towers, wall-mounted units and stone-clad bodies. SAWO also makes combi heaters, steam generators and its own control panels.

## Harvia: key ranges

| Series | Type | Best for |
|---|---|---|

Harvia's control panels (Xenio, Xenio WiFi, Fenix) work with many of its electric heaters; the Wi-Fi version lets you run the sauna from your phone. Some Legend and Cilindro models ship with a digital or Wi-Fi panel included.

SAWO heaters are controlled with Saunova and Innova panels. Ranges such as Tower, Aries and Heaterking also offer high-output options for large saunas and hotels.

## How to choose: five questions

1. **Wood or electric?** If you want wood, Harvia is the easiest route. For electric, both brands are strong.
2. **What's the room volume?** Calculate m³ first (with corrections for glass and stone), then look at series whose range covers it.
3. **What kind of steam?** For soft, full steam choose a large stone mass: Harvia Legend/Cilindro or SAWO Krios/Tower.
4. **Where will the heater go?** On a wall, in a corner, mid-room or inside the bench? SAWO Tower shines mid-room and in benches; Harvia Cilindro Corner suits corners.
5. **How will you control it?** If knobs are enough — Vega/KIP or Nordex/Scandia NB. If you want a timer, display and Wi-Fi — a version with a separate panel.

## FAQ

### Is Harvia better than SAWO?

There's no universal "better". Harvia wins on range breadth and wood stoves; SAWO stands out for its tower designs and electric heaters with large stone capacity. The right choice is the one that fits your sauna.

### Can I use a SAWO control panel with a Harvia heater?

As a rule, each brand's heaters are designed for that brand's control system. Compatibility must be checked for the specific model in the manufacturer's documentation.

### Which is better for a small home sauna?

For 2–6 m³, Harvia Vega/KIP and SAWO Nordex/Scandia are the most common choices. Both are compact and easy to operate.

### Which should I choose for an outdoor sauna?

If you want wood — Harvia (M3, Legend, Linear). For electric, models from either brand work, sized to the room volume.
